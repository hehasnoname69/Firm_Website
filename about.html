OUR STORY

Built on Principle.
Driven by Results.

Justice Law Firm was founded in 1998 by a group of attorneys who believed that every person — regardless of background — deserves fierce, skilled legal representation.

What began as a two-attorney practice on the Lower East Side of Manhattan has grown into one of New York's most respected multi-discipline law firms, with a team of six dedicated specialists covering every major area of civil and personal law.

Over the past 27 years we have recovered more than $50 million for our clients, navigated thousands of complex legal matters, and built a culture defined by transparency, empathy, and relentless advocacy.

WHAT WE DO

Our Six Practice Areas

⚖

Every area is covered by a dedicated specialist — someone whose entire career is focused on that domain.

Family Law

Robert Anderson

Family law cases are among the most emotionally charged legal matters a person will ever face. Our family law team guides clients through divorce proceedings, child custody and support arrangements, adoption, domestic violence protective orders, and post-decree modifications. We combine legal expertise with genuine compassion, always prioritising the wellbeing of your family — especially your children.

  • Divorce & Legal Separation
  • Child Custody & Visitation
  • Child & Spousal Support
  • Adoption & Guardianship
  • Domestic Violence Orders
  • Prenuptial Agreements

Personal Injury

Michael Thompson

When you are injured because of someone else's negligence, you deserve maximum compensation — not just for your medical bills, but for your pain, lost wages, and diminished quality of life. Michael Thompson has recovered over $18 million for accident victims and operates on a strict contingency basis: no fees unless we win.

  • Car & Truck Accidents
  • Slip & Fall Injuries
  • Medical Malpractice
  • Wrongful Death
  • Workplace Injuries
  • Product Liability

Real Estate Law

Sarah Williams

Real estate transactions carry significant financial and legal risk. Sarah Williams has handled over 900 residential and commercial property matters, ensuring every title is clean, every contract is fair, and every client is protected. From first-time buyers to commercial landlords, we are your trusted guide through New York's complex real estate landscape.

  • Residential & Commercial Purchases
  • Title Searches & Insurance
  • Landlord–Tenant Disputes
  • Foreclosure Defense
  • Zoning & Land Use
  • Contract Review & Negotiation

Immigration Law

Jennifer Morgan

Navigating the U.S. immigration system is daunting — one wrong step can have life-altering consequences. Jennifer Morgan has helped over 1,200 clients from 40 countries secure visas, green cards, and citizenship. She is equally skilled in defending removal proceedings and representing asylum seekers in immigration court.

  • Family-Based Green Cards
  • Employment Visas (H-1B, L-1, O-1)
  • Citizenship & Naturalisation
  • Asylum & Refugee Status
  • Deportation & Removal Defense
  • DACA & Special Programs

Intellectual Property Law

Daniel Park

Your ideas, inventions, and creative works are among your most valuable assets. Daniel Park — a former USPTO patent examiner — provides comprehensive IP protection strategies for individuals, startups, and corporations. With over 300 patents filed and numerous successful trademark and copyright registrations, Daniel is the advocate your intellectual property deserves.

  • Patent Application & Prosecution
  • Trademark Registration & Defense
  • Copyright Registration & Licensing
  • Trade Secret Protection
  • IP Litigation & Enforcement
  • Technology & Licensing Agreements

Civil Law

Amara Osei

Civil disputes can arise in every area of life — from broken contracts to civil rights violations. Amara Osei specialises in bringing and defending complex civil actions. She combines meticulous research with compelling courtroom advocacy, ensuring her clients' positions are heard and their rights are protected in every proceeding.

  • Contract Disputes & Breach of Contract
  • Civil Rights Claims (§ 1983)
  • Tort Claims & Negligence
  • Defamation & Slander
  • Employment Discrimination
  • Class Action Representation
